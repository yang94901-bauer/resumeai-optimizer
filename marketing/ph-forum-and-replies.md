## Product Hunt 论坛帖子（发布前或发布日创建）

### 标题
Meet ResumeAI: Turn Chinese resumes into ATS-optimized English in 30 seconds

### 正文
Hey Product Hunt community! 👋

I'm excited to share ResumeAI, a tool I built after seeing countless talented Chinese professionals lose international job opportunities because their English resumes didn't pass ATS filters.

**The Problem**
- 75% of resumes are rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems before a human ever sees them
- Machine translation produces awkward, keyword-poor English that gets filtered out
- Chinese job seekers applying to global companies are at a systematic disadvantage
- Professional resume writers charge $200-500 per resume, out of reach for most

**What ResumeAI Does**
- Paste your Chinese resume → get professional, ATS-optimized English in 30 seconds
- Integrated 500+ industry-specific keywords across tech, finance, marketing, and more
- Generates a matching cover letter tailored to your experience
- Uses action verbs and quantified achievements for native-level quality
- No signup required to try

**How It's Built**
- Backend: Flask + Kimi AI API (Moonshot's LLM, optimized for Chinese-English translation)
- Frontend: Vanilla JS + Tailwind CSS
- License verification: Gumroad API integration
- Deployed on PythonAnywhere + Vercel

**Why This Approach**
I evaluated OpenAI, but as a China-based founder without overseas payment methods, Kimi AI was the practical choice. It handles Chinese-English context exceptionally well and is significantly more cost-effective.

**Roadmap**
- ✅ AI resume optimization + cover letter generation
- ✅ ATS keyword integration
- 🚀 Multi-language support (Japanese, Korean resumes → English)
- 🚀 LinkedIn profile optimization
- 🚀 Resume scoring with actionable feedback
- 🚀 Browser extension for one-click optimization on job boards

**Try it free:** https://yang94901-bauer.github.io/resumeai-optimizer/

Would love to hear your feedback! What features would make this indispensable for your job search?
