# Job Application & Follow-up Email Templates

Use these proven email templates to land interviews and follow up professionally.

---

## 1. Application Follow-up (After 1 Week No Response)

Subject: Following Up on Application - [Job Title]

Dear Hiring Manager,

I submitted my application for the [Job Title] position on [date] and wanted to follow up. I remain highly interested in the role and confident that my [X] years of experience in [field] would add value to [Company Name].

I've attached my resume for your convenience. Please let me know if you need any additional information.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email] | [LinkedIn]

---

## 2. Post-Interview Thank You (Within 24 Hours)

Subject: Thank You - [Job Title] Interview

Dear [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to meet with me today to discuss the [Job Title] role. I enjoyed learning about [Company Name]'s work on [specific topic discussed].

Our conversation reinforced my interest, particularly regarding [specific project/initiative]. I'm confident that my experience in [skill] would allow me to contribute from day one.

I look forward to hearing about next steps. Please don't hesitate to reach out if I can provide any additional information.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 3. Second Interview Follow-up

Subject: Re: [Job Title] - Next Steps

Dear [Recruiter Name],

Thank you for the opportunity to interview with [Interviewer Name] this [day]. I appreciated learning more about the [team/department] and the [specific topic].

I'm excited about the possibility of joining [Company Name] and contributing to [goal]. Based on our discussion, I believe my background in [skill] aligns well with the team's priorities.

Please let me know if there's anything else I can provide. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 4. Offer Negotiation (After Receiving Offer)

Subject: [Job Title] Offer - Discussion

Dear [Recruiter Name],

Thank you for extending the offer for the [Job Title] position. I'm very excited about the opportunity to join [Company Name].

Before I accept, I'd like to discuss the compensation package. Based on my research and [X] years of experience in [field], I was hoping for a base salary in the range of [amount]. I'm also curious about [benefit/title/remote policy].

I'm confident we can find a mutually agreeable arrangement. I'm eager to start contributing to the team.

Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 5. Acceptance Email

Subject: Acceptance - [Job Title] Position

Dear [Recruiter Name],

I'm delighted to accept the offer for the [Job Title] position at [Company Name]. Thank you for this opportunity.

As discussed, my start date will be [date]. Please let me know what onboarding steps I should complete before then.

I'm excited to join the team and contribute to [Company's goal].

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 6. Rejection Response (Keep the Door Open)

Subject: Thank You - [Company Name]

Dear [Recruiter Name],

Thank you for letting me know about your decision. I appreciate the time you and the team invested in the interview process.

I remain very interested in [Company Name] and would welcome the opportunity to be considered for future roles that match my experience in [field].

Wishing you and the team continued success.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 7. Referral Request to Network Contact

Subject: Request - Your Advice

Hi [Name],

I hope you're doing well. I'm currently exploring opportunities in [field] and would value your insight.

I noticed [Company/detail]. Given your experience at [Company], would you be open to a 15-minute call to share your perspective? I'd also be grateful if you know of any open roles that might fit my background in [skill].

Thank you for your time.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Email Etiquette Tips
- Send from professional email (not "[nickname]@gmail.com")
- Subject line: clear and searchable
- Reply within 24 hours to all professional emails
- Proofread - a typo in an email is a red flag
- Keep it under 200 words
- Attach resume as PDF with professional filename
