# 20 English Cover Letter Templates

Customize these templates for different situations. Always tailor to the specific job description (JD).

---

## 1. Standard Application (Experience)

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the [Job Title] position at [Company Name]. With [X] years of experience in [field], I have developed a track record of [key achievement].

In my current role at [Company], I [specific achievement with number]. This experience has equipped me with [skills relevant to JD].

I am particularly drawn to [Company Name] because [reason - research the company]. I believe my expertise in [skill] aligns perfectly with your team's needs.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to [Company Name]'s success. Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

---

## 2. New Graduate / Entry Level

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am a recent graduate of [University] with a degree in [Major]. I am writing to apply for the [Job Title] position at [Company Name].

During my studies, I completed [relevant project/internship] where I [achievement]. This experience developed my [skills]. I am eager to apply my [skill] in a professional setting and grow with [Company Name].

I am a quick learner, detail-oriented, and passionate about [field]. I would appreciate the opportunity to interview for this position.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

---

## 3. Career Change

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the [Job Title] position at [Company Name]. After [X] years in [old field], I am transitioning into [new field], driven by my passion for [reason].

My experience in [old field] has given me transferable skills in [skill 1, skill 2, skill 3]. In particular, my ability to [achievement] is directly relevant to this role.

I have proactively [action taken to learn new field, e.g., completed certification]. I am confident that my fresh perspective combined with proven professional skills would make me a valuable addition to your team.

Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

---

## 4. Referral Mention

Dear [Hiring Manager Name],

[Referrer Name] suggested I reach out regarding the [Job Title] opening at [Company Name]. As [referrer's relationship], they believed my background in [field] would be a strong fit.

I have [X] years of experience in [field], with particular strength in [skill]. At [Company], I [key achievement].

I would be delighted to discuss this opportunity further. Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 5. Follow-up After No Response (2 weeks)

Dear Hiring Manager,

I recently applied for the [Job Title] position at [Company Name] and wanted to follow up on my application. I remain very interested in the role and believe my experience in [skill] would be valuable to your team.

I have attached my resume for your reference. I would be happy to provide any additional information.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

## 7. Short & Direct (Remote Position)

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the [Job Title] remote position. I have [X] years of experience working remotely with distributed teams across [time zones].

At [Company], I [key achievement]. I am highly self-motivated, excellent at async communication, and comfortable with [tools].

I would love to discuss how I can add value to [Company Name]. Thank you.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

## 8. After Interview Thank You

Our conversation reinforced my interest in this role, particularly [specific topic]. I am confident that my experience in [skill] would allow me to contribute immediately.

Please don't hesitate to reach out if you need any additional information. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## 10. Expressing Enthusiasm + Unique Value

I am excited about the possibility of bringing this perspective to [Company Name].

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

## Cover Letter Checklist
- [ ] Addressed to specific person (or "Hiring Manager" if unknown)
- [ ] Mentions the specific company name
- [ ] References the specific role
- [ ] One quantifiable achievement
- [ ] No generic clichés ("hard worker," "team player" without proof)
- [ ] Matches keywords from the job description
- [ ] 200-350 words, 3-4 paragraphs
- [ ] Correct company name spelled right
- [ ] Proofread for typos
