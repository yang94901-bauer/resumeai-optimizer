# LinkedIn Profile Optimization Checklist

Optimize your LinkedIn profile to attract recruiters. Recruiters spend an average of 6 seconds scanning a profile - make every second count.

---

## 1. Headline (Most Important)
- Do NOT just use your job title ("Software Engineer at Company")
- Use value-based headline: "Backend Engineer | Microservices & Cloud | Helped companies cut costs 30%"
- Include 1-2 keywords recruiters search for
- Example: "Data Analyst | SQL • Python • Power BI | Turning Data into Business Decisions"

## 2. Profile Photo
- Professional headshot, plain background, good lighting
- Face takes up 60% of frame
- Smile, business casual attire
- No group photos, no selfies, no cropped wedding photos

## 3. Banner Image
- Use branded banner with your value proposition
- Free tools: Canva has LinkedIn banner templates (1584 x 396 px)

## 4. About Section (3 paragraphs)
- **Paragraph 1 (Hook)**: Who you are + what you do + your "wow" achievement
- **Paragraph 2 (Proof)**: 2-3 quantified achievements with numbers
- **Paragraph 3 (CTA)**: What you're looking for + invite to connect
- Add line breaks between paragraphs (recruiters skim)
- First 2 lines visible without "see more" - put the hook there

## 5. Experience Section
- Don't copy-paste your resume - expand with context
- Use bullet points with action verbs + quantified results
- Include 3-5 bullets per role
- Add media (projects, presentations, portfolio links)

## 6. Skills Section
- Add all 50 skills (max allowed)
- Order matters - put most relevant first
- Ask colleagues to endorse your top skills
- Skills matching job descriptions improve search visibility

## 7. Featured Section
- Pin your best work: portfolio, articles, projects, certifications
- This is the second thing recruiters look at

## 8. Education
- List degrees, honors, relevant coursework
- Add certificates (LinkedIn Learning, Coursera, AWS, etc.)

## 9. Recommendations
- Request 3-5 recommendations from managers, colleagues, clients
- Give recommendations first (people reciprocate)
- Specific recommendations > generic praise

## 10. Activity & Engagement
- Post 2-3 times per week (share insights, not just reposts)
- Comment meaningfully on posts in your field
- Engage with company pages you're interested in
- Recruiters notice active, thoughtful profiles

## 11. Settings Optimization
- Turn ON "Open to Work" (visible to recruiters only)
- Set "Career interests" to receive job alerts
- Enable "Creator mode" if you create content
- Set your location to target job markets

## 12. URL Customization
- Customize your profile URL: linkedin.com/in/yourname
- Put it on your resume and email signature

---

## Profile Formula (Fill in the blanks)

**Headline**: [Role] | [Skill 1] • [Skill 2] • [Skill 3] | [Achievement or Value]

**About - Para 1**: I'm a [Role] with [X] years of experience helping [target audience] [achieve outcome]. Most recently, I [specific quantified achievement].

**About - Para 2**: My expertise spans [skill 1], [skill 2], and [skill 3]. At [Company], I [another achievement with numbers]. Previously at [Company], I [achievement].

**About - Para 3**: I'm currently [what you're doing / looking for]. Let's connect if you're [ideal connection] or interested in [your service]. Message me - I reply to everyone.
